A dedicated, comprehensive study guide and credit research reference repository for candidates preparing for the **AIWMI Certified Credit Research Analyst Level 2 (CCRA-L2) Certification Exam**.

---

## Exam Overview

The AIWMI Certified Credit Research Analyst Level 2 program develops advanced knowledge of credit analysis, rating methodologies, portfolio strategy, credit risk management, and corporate financing structures.

- **Exam Format**: AIWMI's CCRA examination is described as remotely proctored and multiple-choice. Confirm the specific Level 2 delivery arrangements with the provider before scheduling.
- **Target Audience**: Credit analysts, banking professionals, financial analysts, rating professionals, portfolio managers, and finance graduates.
- **Prerequisites**: No specific Level 2 prerequisite was confirmed in the available provider information. Candidates should check the current admission requirements with AIWMI.

---

## Core Exam Domains & Knowledge Areas

### 1. Credit Rating – Internal and External

- Credit rating fundamentals, credit risk management, and the 5C credit analysis model.
- Industry, business, financial, management, and project risk assessment.
- Rating methodologies for manufacturing companies, financial institutions, banks, and sovereign entities.

### 2. Credit Strategy and Portfolio Management

- Yield curves, interest rate risk, and credit spread analysis.
- Distressed debt, systemic risk, and multi-asset-class credit portfolios.
- Emerging-market trading strategies and credit portfolio analysis.

### 3. Credit Monitoring, NPA Management, Enhancement and Securitization

- Credit monitoring, early warning signals, and stressed asset management.
- Credit enhancement structures and recovery mechanisms.
- Asset-backed and mortgage-backed securitization analysis and ratings.

### 4. Credit Risk Models and Regulations

- Credit rating agency regulations and debt guidelines.
- Credit risk models, including Merton models and related analytical methods.
- Regression, time-series modeling, and quantitative credit risk analysis.

### 5. Corporate Banking Facilities and Financing Structures

- Corporate banking facilities and working capital finance assessment.
- Treasury instruments, corporate papers, and financing structures.
- Credit analysis across NBFCs, private banking, asset reconstruction, priority-sector lending, and consortium lending.

## Recommended Practice & Study Resources

To prepare effectively for CCRA Level 2, combine the program's study materials with credit research, financial analysis, and practical risk assessment.

- **CertsGate**: Explore [AIWMI CCRA-L2 preparation resources](https://certsgate.com/exams/aiwmi/ccra-l2) for supplementary study materials and practice questions.
- **Practical Credit Research**: Apply credit appraisal methods to company financial statements, study RBI and SEBI regulatory guidance, review Basel credit-risk principles, and practice analyzing ratings, yield curves, credit spreads, and stressed assets.
